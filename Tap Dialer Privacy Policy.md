# Tap Dialer Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 7 October 2026
**App:** Tap Dialer (Android package `com.smmooth.tapdialer`)
**Contact:** sbvt88@gmail.com

## In short

Tap Dialer does not collect, store on any server, share, or sell your personal information. There is no account, no advertising, and no analytics or tracking. Everything the app does happens on your phone. The only data the app keeps is a few settings and counters on your own device, listed below.

## Who we are

Tap Dialer is published by its developer ("we", "us", "our"). You can reach us at **sbvt88@gmail.com** with any question about this policy or the app.

## What the app does with your information

Tap Dialer lets you type a phone number and then tap an icon to call it on a chosen SIM, open a text message to it, or open a WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business chat, voice call, or video call with it.

| Information | What we do with it |
| --- | --- |
| **The phone number you type** | Held in the app's memory only while the app is open. It is not saved to storage and is gone when the app closes. When you tap an action icon, the number is handed to Android's phone or messaging system, or to the WhatsApp / WhatsApp Business app you chose, so that action can happen. It is never sent to us. |
| **Your contacts** | Only if you tap **WA Call**, **WA Video**, or their WhatsApp Business versions, the app looks in your contacts for the call entry that WhatsApp keeps for that one number, so the call can start straight away. The result is used immediately and is not stored, copied, or sent anywhere. If you do not allow contacts access, the chat opens instead. |
| **Phone and SIM information** | Used only when you tap **SIM 1** or **SIM 2**, to place the call and to work out which SIM is which. No phone number of yours, SIM identifier, or device identifier is stored or sent anywhere. |
| **The clipboard** | Read only when you choose **Paste**, and written only when you choose **Copy**. The app never reads the clipboard in the background. |
| **Your settings** | Which icons are shown, their order, and any names you give SIM 1 and SIM 2 (for example "Work"). Stored on your device only. |
| **Purchase status** | If you buy the WA-B unlock, the app keeps a note on your device that it is unlocked and when that was last confirmed with Google Play, so it works offline. |
| **Rating reminder counters** | A count of how many actions have worked and the date of the last reminder, stored on your device, so the app can ask for a Google Play rating at a sensible time and not too often. |

## What we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address, location, contact list, call history, messages, advertising ID, or any device identifier. The app does not read your text messages or call log, does not send text messages by itself (it only opens your messaging app), and does not use accessibility services. The app contains no advertising or analytics software, and it has no server or back end of its own that your data could be sent to.

## Permissions the app asks for

| Permission | Why |
| --- | --- |
| **Phone** (make calls, read phone state) | To place a call on the SIM you chose and to tell SIM 1 from SIM 2. Used only when you tap a call icon. |
| **Contacts** (read contacts) | To start a WhatsApp voice or video call for a number you have saved. Used only when you tap a WhatsApp call or video icon. Optional: without it, the chat opens instead. |

You can change these at any time in Android Settings > Apps > Tap Dialer > Permissions.

## Third parties

- **Google Play.** The app is distributed through Google Play. If you buy the WA-B unlock, Google Play handles the payment and we receive no payment details; the app only asks Google Play whether you own the unlock. If you choose to rate the app, the rating window is shown and handled by Google. Google may also give us anonymous, combined crash and performance statistics (Android vitals) from users who have chosen to share diagnostics with Google. Google's own privacy policy applies to that: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- **WhatsApp and WhatsApp Business.** When you tap one of the WhatsApp icons, the number you typed is passed to the WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business app on your phone. What those apps do with it is covered by their own terms and privacy policies. Tap Dialer is an independent app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by WhatsApp LLC or Meta Platforms, Inc. WhatsApp is a trademark of its owner.
- **Your phone's calling and messaging apps and your mobile carrier.** Calls and texts are handled by Android, your messaging app, and your carrier under their own terms.

We do not sell your information and we do not share it with anyone, because we do not have it.

## How long data is kept and how to remove it

The settings, purchase note, and counters described above stay on your device until you remove them. Uninstalling Tap Dialer, or clearing its storage in Android Settings > Apps > Tap Dialer > Storage, deletes all of it. The phone number you type is never saved.

## Security

Because Tap Dialer sends no personal data to us or to anyone else, there is no stored copy of your data for us to protect. The data it keeps on your device is protected by Android's normal app protections.

## Children

Tap Dialer is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.
